# A01-S02 — THE MECHANICALS' IMPOSSIBLE PLAY
## Act I, Scene 2 — screenplay / performance draft

**Location:** Old fabrication workshop, beneath the Dyson sphere's public festival stage.
**Dramatic arc:** Working robots volunteer to perform an earnest, hopelessly ambitious tragedy at the royal ceremony.
**Visual:** The six performers are distinct old-school robots. Their gears, valves, bellows, hand tools and manual controls remain visibly functional. No human disguise. Their theater machinery uses crank handles, hologram projectors and unreliable stage modules.

### Beat 1 — All parts available, none guaranteed

[PETER QUINCE, a tall carpenter robot, pins a paper casting list to a workbench. NICK BOTTOM, a broad, energetic weaver robot, emerges trailing thread. FRANCIS FLUTE tends a pressure bellows. TOM SNOUT has a soldering tool; SNUG carries massive joinery clamps; ROBIN STARVELING stitches a costume with a delicate precision hand.]

QUINCE — he:
Hands off the scenery. We have been invited to entertain the Commander's wedding. The competition includes professional choirs and a gravity ballet. We have a hammer and six volunteers.

BOTTOM — he:
Seven, if I count my spare voice box.

QUINCE — he:
I refuse to count that box. It keeps singing through the safety announcements.

BOTTOM — he:
A gift is a gift. What masterpiece are we presenting?

QUINCE — he:
The sorrowful tale of Pyramus and Thisbe. Two lovers divided by a wall.

SNOUT — he:
What kind of wall? I can make it load-bearing.

QUINCE — he:
A theatrical wall.

SNOUT — he:
Ah. The dangerous kind.

### Beat 2 — Casting disaster

QUINCE — he:
Bottom, you are Pyramus. Our great tragic lover.

BOTTOM — he:
An excellent choice. I can also play Thisbe, the wall, and grief itself. I have facial plates for grief.

QUINCE — he:
Flute will play Thisbe.

FLUTE — he:
My throat module has a crack. I sound like an airlock losing pressure.

BOTTOM — he:
Then she will sound heartbreakingly sincere. Unless you want me to do it.

QUINCE — he:
I do not.

QUINCE — he:
Snout, you are the wall.

SNOUT — he:
A speaking wall?

QUINCE — he:
One with a crack for the lovers to whisper through.

SNOUT — he:
Finally. A structural defect with dialogue.

QUINCE — he:
Snug, you play the lion.

SNUG — he:
Do I get words?

QUINCE — he:
Only a roar.

SNUG — he:
I can roar at three settings: reassuring, industrial, and catastrophic.

QUINCE — he:
Reassuring. We cannot frighten the Commander into canceling the wedding.

BOTTOM — he:
Let me play the lion! I can roar and weep and die, all in one breath.

QUINCE — he:
And then who plays Pyramus?

BOTTOM — he:
A difficult production choice. I respect your burden.

QUINCE — he:
Starveling, you are moonlight.

STARVELING — he:
At last, a part with a steady power supply.

### Beat 3 — The rehearsal site

[QUINCE unfolds a map of the sphere's lower habitats.]

QUINCE — he:
Tomorrow we rehearse in the old garden. No patrons, no inspectors, no audience to discover our mistakes before opening night.

SNOUT — he:
The garden with the wild wiring?

FLUTE — he:
And the strange voices in the pipes?

BOTTOM — he:
Even better! Real atmosphere. An artist needs danger.

SNUG — he:
I would prefer the kind of danger that can be switched off.

QUINCE — he:
Bring your scripts, your tools, and any replacement parts you can carry.

BOTTOM — he:
And a mirror. Tragedy requires a magnificent face.

[The other five look at his riveted face. BOTTOM polishes it lovingly. Beyond the workshop door, the forgotten garden flickers with small, moving crystal lights.]

**END A01-S02.** Continuity out: the six mechanicals have agreed to rehearse in the same garden toward which the lovers are traveling.
