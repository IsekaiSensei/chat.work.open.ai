### A. Design rule — gem is defined by the setting








- **Warm stones**: Garnet, Ruby, Citrine, Yellow Topaz, Yellow Sapphire, Padparadscha, Fire Opal, Carnelian — preferred warm frame language: yellow gold, rose gold, brass gold, rainbow titanium biased toward gold/rose/copper, bronze titanium.

- **Clear / neutral / high-brilliance stones**: Diamond, Clear Quartz, Moonstone, Pearl, White Opal — material setting defines mood: white metal = cool/icy modern; yellow gold = warm/vintage; rose gold = warm/romantic. Any can be used if the individual owner's reference requires it.
