## Mission architecture — Moon to Oort

The Moon is the practical proving ground in the story's scale ladder. The longer horizon is a self-expanding network of orbital and deep-space infrastructure:

1. Commission the ship and establish crew command, data, and production systems.
2. Build a Moon Yard: a testable lunar resource, fabrication, power, maintenance, and logistics capability.
3. Develop the seed constructor. Distinguish copying a blueprint, fabricating a machine, constructing a self-replicating system, and deploying a new probe.
4. Establish orbital depots, power and communications relays, and local resource operations.
5. Extend through asteroid and outer-system infrastructure.
6. Send the Von Neumann team toward the Oort Cloud, where local resources, energy, control, and replication constraints become central.

A seed is not automatically a factory. A factory is not automatically self-replicating. A copied blueprint is not a fabricated machine. A power relay is not automatically a communications relay. Keep the ambition large and the engineering reasoning honest.

## Film and dialogue workflow

Use the [GERTECH Dialogue Extractor Protocol](GERTECH_DIALOGUE_EXTRACTOR_PROTOCOL_v1.md), [CSV register](GERTECH_DIALOGUE_EXTRACTION_TEMPLATE.csv), and [JSON register](GERTECH_DIALOGUE_EXTRACTION_TEMPLATE.json).

1. Raw sweep: extract exact dialogue in original order, with source location and speaker.
2. Distinction filter: preserve exact statements that carry the lore or decision.
3. Scene formatting: put selected quotations into the requested cinematic structure without rewriting them.
4. Visual assembly: use established references and verify actual media outputs.

Keep source speaker separate from on-screen performer. Preserve wording, repetitions, corrections, and context. Do not attribute example lines or AI-written sentences to a historical speaker. Do not summarize when asked to extract. Ten-second timing must be checked against actual audio; every ten-second render must be complete and independently satisfying.

Keep the evidence chain distinct: source dialogue → extraction → distinction review → storyboard → generation prompt → rendered clip → edited film → published result. A written scene is not a render; a render is not an edit; an edit is not a published post.
