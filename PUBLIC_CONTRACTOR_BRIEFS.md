## What completion means

- **PSA:** ten seconds total, one clear point, clean pause before and after inside those ten seconds. Keep the exact source picture and submitted animation prompt together. Audio belongs in the storyboard and timing, not only at the end.
- **Short film / animatic:** a complete scene and honest medium label. Its duration follows the scene; it is not automatically a ten-second PSA. Name a definite camera angle, one visible action and a definite ending for each generation window.
- **Godot or HTML game:** a complete usable package with all dependencies, controls, meaningful interaction, reachable outcome and restart. A screenshot, lone node, missing asset list or explanation is intermediate work.
- **Art/audio:** actual editable output files, dependencies, credits and one complete inspection/playback example. A still does not prove a 3D mesh; a cue list does not prove audio files.
