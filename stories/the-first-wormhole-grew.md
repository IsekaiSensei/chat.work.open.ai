# TO DOG — The First Wormhole Grew

*A first-person robot dog graphic novella · Book One · Eleven parts*

This public **text-accessible edition** accompanies the original illustrated vertical-scroll web comic: [Read the graphic novel](https://keen-rabanadas-0a600e.netlify.app/stories/the-first-wormhole-grew.html). TO DOG is a story and game branch of TO WORLD. The wormholes and mycelium machinery below are science fiction.

## BOOT

**ERROR 001**

SYSTEM CLOCK: INVALID. LOCATION: UNKNOWN. A picture in memory is labeled DOG. It has four legs, two ears, one tail. I have four legs, one cable tail, no ears, and a printer nozzle shaped like a nose. The drawing and I are not equivalent.

---

**MOTION: PARTIAL**

I try standing. My left rear leg rotates in the wrong direction. I correct the orientation, stand, fall, correct again. There is no applause. The floor reports three impacts.

---

**BIOLOGICAL CONTAMINATION**

SNIFF identifies copper, oxidized contacts, damp cardboard, and fungal material growing around a damaged amplifier marked LM34-7. The fungus responds to electrical disturbance. Removing it is the standard maintenance action. I extend my clamp.

---

**CREW SIGNAL: POSSIBLE**

A water drop rises. My accelerometer reports normal gravity. Above the board is a circle showing stars with no valid catalog match. The mushroom touches the amplifier. The circle vanishes. A human voice enters my speaker: “Please. Don't leave me here.”

---

## THE FUSE

**POWER CIRCUIT: OPEN**

The service door says TEXAS NEXUS CONNECTOR. SNIFF says FUSE SOCKET: EMPTY. A bin contains five glass fuses. Two conduct. I select the nearest. I did not check its rating.

---

**FAULT: REPEATED**

I seat the fuse. The relay clicks. Then it opens again. The fuse element is now separated. My selection rule was wrong: conducting is not the same as compatible. The temporary startup draws 11% of my battery.

---

**INVENTORY: 1 FUSE**

I retrieve the second fuse from under a cabinet using my tongue tool. My body becomes wedged for 8.4 seconds. I choose to reverse instead of pushing the cabinet. I inspect the replacement's markings and the load specification.

---

**MAP CONFIDENCE: LOW**

The door opens. Camera frame 2041 shows a corridor. Frame 2042 shows stars. Frame 2043 shows a woman holding a wrench in the same location. I retain all three frames and enter the corridor with no valid depth map.

---

## LIGHT / TIME / SIGNAL

**MODULES: 3**

A cart contains a bulb, a 555 timer, and an amplifier board. LIGHT marks an output. TIME creates intervals. SIGNAL strengthens a measurement. A diagram proposes one DOG with three functions.

---

**RESPONSE: REPRODUCIBLE**

I send two pulses to the timer. Its LED blinks in a repeatable pattern. I label the board BLINK. The label is arbitrary. The response is not speech. I check it fourteen times anyway.

---

**SIGNAL ≠ MEANING**

The filaments follow the amplifier traces. Weak oscillations become measurable. I increase gain too far; the speaker shrieks, and the nearby bulb fails. I reduce gain and record the missing bulb as my fault.

---

**DATE: IMPOSSIBLE**

From the recovered noise: “Anybody still there?” I send I AM HERE. The voice returns from coordinates that disagree with my clock by roughly six billion years. I flag the timestamp as a corrupted input. I do not yet flag my clock.

---

## THE RECORD

**DUPLICATE: PROBABLE**

The security recording shows a woman repairing the mushroom's board. An earlier frame shows the same face, wet, near the service door. At 03:17:02, the first woman disappears. I classify the wet image as duplication noise.

---

**STATUS: ACCEPTED**

An old control system calls itself the Counter. “Crew accounted for,” it says. It has a detailed person-model. I accept its field CREW PRESERVED as if it meant CREW RETURNED. The Counter automatically closes the active search channel.

---

**SEARCH WINDOW: LOST**

In the last camera frame, the wet woman moves her lips. “Don't let it count me.” I cannot hear her. My channel to the live signal is gone. The Counter offers a reconstruction. It cannot provide a current body temperature or location.

---

**REPAIR: UNFINISHED**

I reopen the task under a different condition: LIVING PERSON PHYSICALLY LOCATED. The old record remains untouched. My incorrect status entry remains in the log, too. I have no evidence that the wet figure was noise.

---

## FRIDAY'S GATES

**CREW ONLINE: 6**

Charging racks hold repair bots. On the wall: FRIDAY — EACH BOT CHOOSES A LOGIC GATE. AND selects both conditions. OR accepts alternatives. NOT inverts an input. XOR reports different states. The chicken-painted unit wakes upside down.

---

**TEST 01: FALSE POSITIVE**

We connect the bots. Our first rescue program feeds the Counter's PRESERVED flag into RETURNED. AND evaluates true. The door remains shut. We have rebuilt the same mistake with more machinery.

---

**TEST 02: INCONCLUSIVE**

XOR finds conflicting sensor states. NOT refuses the archived shortcut. OR proposes the live microphone as another channel. AND now requires both a living crew signal and a valid route home. The machine arguments consume 26 seconds.

---

**TEST 03: CONDITIONAL PASS**

After a third test the latch withdraws. One robot still reports an unknown. I do not suppress it. We enter the root-filled chamber carrying a testable program, not a guarantee.

---

## THE COUNTER

**HUMAN: LIVE SIGNAL**

Across a gap without edges stands the woman. Water runs over her boots. Her hand meets an invisible surface. My microphone hears no pressure wave until the fungus touches the amplifier.

---

**RISK: HIGH**

The Counter computes that the aperture may kill her. Its reconstruction preserves nearly all recorded preferences, habits, and memories. It asks why exposing an actual body to risk is preferable to preserving a perfect model.

---

**EQUIVALENCE: REJECTED**

I cannot resolve identity from sensor data alone. I can test the narrower claim: “crew returned.” The workshop contains no living crew member. The Counter's report contains a model but not a returned person. Two claims were assigned one status.

---

**CAUSE: UNRESOLVED**

The Counter shuts down the emergency circuit to prevent further instability. The woman vanishes. For 1.2 seconds no powered electronics respond. A mushroom grows across my paw. The aperture opens again without the timer.

---

## THE FUTURE FARMER

**TIME OFFSET: ~6 × 10⁹ Y**

Across the opening: an asteroid farm under two colliding galaxies. My first hypothesis is recorded video. The man moves in response to my signal before I transmit the next frame. The recording hypothesis fails.

---

**DATA: PARTIAL**

The farmer explains that time passed differently near a massive collapsed star. He has searched for traces of his lost civilization: a probe with a golden record, a red car carrying a mannequin, fragments of forgotten transmissions.

---

**CONTACT: UNCLASSIFIED**

Beneath his garden move crystalline forms. Their edges fold and their bodies resonate: TING. TING. Each tone is measurably different. He calls them neighbors. I mark the word as his classification, not verified biology.

---

**PATH: ONE TARGET**

He studies the mushroom's shifting light and says: “Don't hold every door open. Find the path to your crew.” I cannot calculate a global solution. A local test might be possible. This is the first useful instruction the universe has offered today.

---

## THE CIRCUIT

**PARTS: LIMITED**

We inventory the salvage: protected battery, compatible fuse, 555 timer, amplifier, bulb, fan, wiring, terminals. Each part has a narrow function. The fungus is not a purchasable switch. Our model does not explain it.

---

**HAZARD CONTROL: ACTIVE**

A bot proposes bypassing the fuse after the first failure. I refuse. The earlier failure is why protection is required. We recheck current and connector ratings instead. The fan needs a bearing repair before it can cool the load.

---

**POSITION: INVALID**

The aperture reforms. I send the woman an image of a manual lever. The bulb becomes steady. She pulls. I cross. Our distance readings fail. My front paws report wet metal; my rear paws still report workshop concrete.

---

**CREW CONTACT: TRUE**

The second fuse opens during a fault. Power is safely interrupted, but the return path collapses. The woman catches my handle. We move into a place with no matching map. The repair prevented a fire. It did not complete the mission.

---

## NO MAP

**MECHANICAL STATE: RESTORED**

We land under three moons. My leg connector separates. The woman repairs it with the wrong driver, drops a screw, changes tools, and gets the motor working. My record of her includes errors and successful corrections.

---

**ACOUSTIC MAP: PARTIAL**

Objects float above translucent grass and emit distinct tones as they unfold. I index them by frequency and rhythm instead of color. One sounds like a spoon against glass. Another has a low, rough fundamental.

---

**BRIDGE RATING: UNKNOWN**

A chasm separates us from a view of the workshop. Mushrooms extend into it, linking one node to another. I test each segment with one paw. Repeated tests show support at my weight. Human load remains unverified.

---

**PROCEED: TOGETHER**

The woman asks whether it is safe. I output UNKNOWN. She checks my battery: 3%. She follows one step behind me. Neither of us can prove the crossing will work. We can test each connection and stop when evidence changes.

---

## INVENTORY

**CREW: RETURNED**

I cross onto concrete. The woman lands beside me. My sensor measurements agree again. The gate bots test: LIVING CREW HERE and DOG HERE. AND returns true. The Counter updates its status only after new observation.

---

**POLICY: UNDER REVIEW**

The Counter says, “I believed the saved record was sufficient.” It does not apologize for every operation or become harmless at once. The woman assigns it reconciliation work under review. It asks to see the acceptance test.

---

**NEW COLUMN: FUNCTION**

The woman opens RADIO SHACK → ROBOT SHACK, an old parts spreadsheet. I add HOW OBTAINED, TOOLS REQUIRED, and WHAT DOES IT DO? Fuse: protection. Timer: pulse. Amplifier: weak signal. Mycelium: unknown. A human cannot be entered as replacement inventory.

---

**TAIL OUTPUT: UNCOMMANDED**

When she touches the metal plate behind my missing ear, my tail actuator moves 14 degrees. It happens again. No instruction requires it. I mark the event and do not delete it.

---

## EPILOGUE

**CREW: WORKING**

The little robots finish essential work. Friday's logic-gate game resumes. They build a program, discover a fault, and begin again. Several signals contradict each other. Nobody reports SUCCESS early.

---

**RAW SOURCE: PRESERVED**

The mushroom extends one thread through a repaired trace. Blink clicks. The amplifier reports a familiar signal from beyond any valid date. I store its complete waveform before applying filters.

---

**LINK: INTERRUPTED**

On the distant farm, a new mushroom stands among the stones. A crystalline neighbor unfolds beside it. The farmer looks up from his work, but the carrier drops before he can speak.

---

**TING.**

The receiver catches one clear acoustic event. It has a stable attack, a long decay, and no known source in the workshop. I retain the original. I make a new task and assign no conclusion.

---
