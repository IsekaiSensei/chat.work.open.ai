## 1. Active assignment

Readapt *A Midsummer Night's Dream* section by section as a dramatic production set inside a functioning, surreal GERTECH Dyson sphere.

- Write playable scenes with actual dialogue and, where useful, lyrics; do not substitute plot summaries.
- Preserve the original scene's dramatic purpose, relationships, conflicts, emotional progression, humor, double meanings, and payoffs.
- Transform the dialogue, actions, setting and metaphors to fit fabrication, cybernetics, regeneration, crystal engineering, energy systems, and ether relays.
- The source play remains unchanged. Keep the new adaptation clearly separate from it.

## 2. Characters, construction and factions

- **Athenians:** visibly engineered cyborgs; varied degrees of organic and mechanical construction.
- **Fairies:** believable adult crystal beings, not ordinary humans in costumes. Their structural, energetic and outer-body layers follow the selected character art.
- **Mechanicals:** recognizable old-school, visibly constructed robots, with distinct mechanical personalities and working trades.
- Across these groups, origins matter: **printed, made, built, made by nature, regenerated through artifice**. Origin shapes identity, status, allegiance, prejudice, love, authority and belonging.
- Give characters different silhouettes, materials, histories and means of movement. Do not flatten the cast into one generic model.
- Fairy portrayals are adults **25+**. Isekai Sensei is **40** when included, unless the owner directs otherwise.

## 3. Visual authority and art direction

- Only **owner-reviewed and selected art** becomes the primary visual reference. A supplied or newly generated image is a candidate until selected.
- **FIRST MODEL** means the original selected image for this production and character, never an image from a later render.
- Detailed anatomy, materials, clothing, equipment, pose and reading instructions belong **on the individual art pages**, not in this global orders file.
- Keep the Dyson sphere, its habitats, machinery and relay infrastructure visually coherent throughout each scene.

## 4. Script and source handling

- Before working from a source, read the exact identified source file. Treat it as read-only.
- **Adaptation** is authorized for the designated source section; author it as a separately named new script. Never overwrite the source.
- Once a production script is selected as the working master, **sectioning and batching are copy operations, not rewrites**. Preserve its exact speaker, dialogue, lyrics, punctuation, stage actions, music cues and sequence.
- Give each master row one stable source ID. Assign it to one batch only; no missing, overlapping, repeated or silently altered rows.
- Check section exports against the selected master before a render handoff. If a comparison fails or the master is unavailable, stop that handoff and identify the problem.
- An explicit owner instruction to alter, adapt or renumber material applies only to the content named in that instruction.

## 5. Render execution

- **Batch size:** 10 consecutive clips; the final batch may be shorter.
- **Timing:** around 8 seconds is typical, 10 seconds is a target rather than a forced duration; allow 6–12 seconds as dialogue and music require. Prefer natural phrase boundaries.
- Immediately precede each dialogue or SING line with the named performer and the appropriate **he:/she:/they:** label. Only that performer mouths the line.
- In **every render job**, attach/reference that character's actual selected JPEG and explicitly instruct **FORCE FIRST MODEL** using that original source. Do not derive identity from the preceding render.
- Preserve musical, visual and character continuity across consecutive clips.
- When the owner says **run, render, go, or proceed** for an identified scope, process its ordered batches without asking for a second GO. Do not claim a video was rendered unless a video render is verified.
- Keep rendered, pending, rejected and unknown statuses distinct. Render-status reports end with **LAST RENDERED CLIP: NNNN** or **LAST RENDERED CLIP: NONE**, whichever is verified.
