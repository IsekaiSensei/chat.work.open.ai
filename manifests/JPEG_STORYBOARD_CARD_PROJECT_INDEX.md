# JPEG → STORYBOARD CARD — PRODUCTION INDEX
Creator: Isekai Ryan
Current stage: actual source-inventory listing, NOT rendered-card inventory. Clean source JPEGs stay unchanged.

**ONE WINDOW / ONE CARD:** Each original image gets its OWN derived storyboard JPEG. On the storyboard sheet, include the original reference image and the exact 1–9 parts in a readable strip/sidebar. The original image stays entirely text-free. Do not write the storyboard directions into the character or concept art itself.

**FOREGROUND + MESH:** Use the owner-selected attractive adult character as the visually dominant foreground subject. Put a low-contrast mechanical mesh/lattice in the background. For FACE CYCLING, preserve the actual source face and demonstrate seven head angles across 180 degrees: left profile (-90°), left 60°, left 30°, front 0°, right 30°, right 60°, right profile (+90°). These angle designations are on the storyboard strip only. The rendered character/image/animation contains no letters or face markings. Only animate poses genuinely supported by the accepted identity reference.

**FROM CARD TO BOOK:** A card is one sourced prompt + its source image + its exact real action. Repair that card individually. Arrange accepted cards in ordered PAGES. Test each PAGE-to-PAGE transition and timing as a SCROLL. Promote to BOOK only if complete continuity, source identities, and original audio agree. A ten-blink GIF, short, or slideshow is a playback/export choice, not proof a scroll works.

**PRODUCTION-SPECIFIC TEXT:** The Gremlin PSA's tested one-window STORYBOARD sheets may contain numbered written instructions, dialogue, and shot order. Do not apply its graphic/text overlay look to general concept art. For ordinary concept pages, there is no printed title, label, number, letter, honeycomb alphabet symbol, technical callout, or paragraph. Traditional cartoon squash/stretch/anime physical freedoms can inform the pocket-world scenes without replacing the locked source model.

**QUEEN BEE FAILURE MODE:** Use honeycomb HEXAGON GEOMETRY, actual bees/wing vein structure and gold/amber jewelry. The bee's body, jewelry, floor and wings should not be filled with the letter 'B', repeated letters, or printed honeycomb labels. Naming 'Queen Bee' on an instructional sheet does NOT instruct the renderer to draw text in her image.
