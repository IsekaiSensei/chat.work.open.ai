# TO WORLD — AUTHORSHIP, IDENTITY & PROVENANCE STANDARD

**Authority:** Captain's clarification, November 22, 2026  
**Applies to:** Every public wiki entry and every published or derived TO WORLD / GERTECH work: canon, writing, dialogue, images, likenesses, films, audio, code, games, mods, 3D models, STL files, patterns, fabrication plans, and social-media presentations.

## Governing principle

**Preserve authorship. Do not take the Captain out of his own work.**

Ryan is the originating author, creator, director, or source of the ideas and decisions he contributes. Preserve that contribution accurately. Do not replace it with an AI attribution, omit it during cleanup, or imply that an AI independently originated the Captain's concept, canon, identity, or likeness.

Authorship tracking is not the same as claiming that every production component was made by one person. Record each contribution truthfully: the Captain's originating idea and direction, human collaborators, AI assistance, tools used, and the actual production or fabrication process.

- Treat Ryan's real name and face/likeness as protected identity and authorship attributes.
- Preserve correct identity and source attribution in private provenance records. Never invent, substitute, anonymize, misspell, or silently remove the Captain's real name or likeness from the authorship record.

## Required provenance for each published artifact

Keep a compact record with the work, either in the page metadata or its linked release record:

1. **Artifact identity:** stable ID, title, media/file type, version, and date.
2. **Origin:** who supplied the originating concept, text, design, reference, or direction.
3. **Authorship and contributors:** Ryan's specific contribution; any human collaborators; each AI/tool's actual contribution (research, drafting, editing, image generation, code assistance, rendering, etc.).
4. **Identity/likeness:** whether Ryan's name, face, voice, or other identifiable personal material is used; the approved source and scope.
5. **Source lineage:** private source reference retained in Drive, parent version, and the changes that produced this public release. Do not expose private Drive links or private source IDs in the public record.
6. **Credit and permission:** the Captain-approved public byline/credit, publication approval, and any limits on reuse or redistribution.
7. **Release destination:** public page, repository path, social post, video, or downloadable artifact, with a working link where available.

Do not fill unknown fields by guessing. Mark them unknown or pending. A generated image is not automatically a finished model; a model is not automatically a tested print; a script is not a rendered film; a social embed is not a transfer of the source media.

## Public-release boundary

Google Drive remains the complete private and working source library. The public GitHub repository and GERTECH Wiki contain only already-public/published material or material the Captain explicitly approves for public release.

Before publishing, check authorship, identity, source lineage, and approved credit. Remove unrelated private information when necessary, but do not confuse privacy protection with stripping the Captain's authorship, face, real name, or public canon. Keep untouched originals and full production history in Drive.

## Correction rule

When attribution, likeness, identity, location treatment, or source lineage is wrong or missing, correct the public record and preserve the change history. Do not silently rewrite the original source. Raise a genuine ambiguity to the Captain; do not decide on his behalf to erase or reassign authorship.
