PILOT-RF-OPEN-001 — NORTHBOUND
A black Saturn Astra XE five-door hatchback travels north on I-5 with ordinary morning traffic. Ryan drives. The car keeps its lane and a steady pace as the freeway curves toward San Diego.

PILOT-RF-OPEN-002 — THE DRIVER
Inside the same car, Ryan eases off as the vehicle ahead slows. He maintains the space, then resumes the traffic's pace. His attention stays on the road.

PILOT-RF-OPEN-003 — THE APPROACH
The Astra takes the Coronado Bridge approach. The rising bridge is visible beyond the turn. Ryan stays on the approach before the climb.

PILOT-RF-OPEN-004 — OUT OF SIGHT
Ryan rounds the concealed bend. Ordinary concrete hides the Astra from the traffic behind. The following car continues along its own visible route, unaware of the hidden lane.

PILOT-RF-OPEN-005 — THE SLAB
On the hidden side of the bend, the Astra rolls onto a level concrete road slab. Ryan brakes gently. All four tires settle within the slab's perimeter. A narrow rectangular seam becomes visible around the stationary car.

PILOT-RF-OPEN-006 — DOWN
The concrete slab lowers while remaining level. It carries the whole car into the shaft. Daylight moves upward and closes above. The platform meets the lower road and stops flush with it. Ryan and the Astra remain on the platform.

PILOT-RF-OPEN-007 — BELOW THE BAY
Ryan drives off the settled slab into a dry under-bay road tube. Blue water lies beyond reinforced viewing panes. Service pipes and pump housings flank the road. The car follows a gentle curve into the submerged route.

PILOT-RF-OPEN-008 — AGUA ARRIVAL
The Astra rolls from the under-bay tube into a dry arrival bay at Agua World / Secret Bay Aqua Facility. Blue water is visible beyond the observation glass. Ryan pulls into a marked stopping space and brings the same car to rest. He remains seated for a moment.
