## Society / faction law
Public origin labels inside the Dyson sphere:
- **PRINTED** — grown or printed from licensed templates; some carry inherited or disputed fabrication contracts.
- **MADE** — assembled or synthesized into personhood by fabrication houses.
- **BUILT** — hand-constructed or incrementally rebuilt by a craft lineage; can include cyborgs and robots.
- **BORN / MADE BY NATURE** — organically born people, commonly augmented for station life.
- **REGENERATED** — reconstructed from earlier life, retained memory, or a recovered seed.

These are *social claims*, not proof of a person's worth. Prejudice and law misuse them. The story challenges that ranking. Athenians appear as varied cyborgs; the ether-court characters are now marble mana people, fabricated by machines and marble throughout; the mechanicals are clearly old-school working robots. Casting labels in drafts guide the conflict, **not** the selection of final visual assets. The owner selects FIRST MODEL after art review.

## Scene workflow

1. Read the source scene and develop an original adaptation in the matching scene file.
2. Keep all spoken words, stage action, music cues and character identity together in the script, **not** in ORDERS.md.
3. Owner selects scene script and reference art. That selected script becomes the immutable batch source; adaptation drafting does not authorize a renderer to improvise dialogue.
4. Only then segment by performance beats into ordered ten-clip groups (short final group allowed), using 6–12 seconds as suitable.
5. Any further change to selected dialogue must be explicit and must create a traceable revision without overwriting the source play.
