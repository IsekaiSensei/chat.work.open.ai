# Bring Your Own Agent — Fairy Identity and Art Direction

Status: RYAN DIRECTION / DESIGN PROPOSAL; integration and provider permissions unverified. Public, task-scoped design brief. No private project history is required.

## Player promise
A complete fairy companion is included with the game. Do not sell fairy access as an extra. A player may choose a built-in fairy or, where a provider supports an authorized connection, bring their existing assistant. **Provider account costs and APIs are not controlled by this game**; no claim that every provider integrates today.

## Identity versus connectivity
- **Fairy appearance:** a visual embodiment selected or customized by the player, reflecting the assistant's style without pretending an unlicensed likeness or brand is official.
- **Agent adapter:** optional permissioned bridge to a supported external assistant. The fairy remains usable without the bridge.
- **Identity data:** provider choice, chosen appearance, voice preference where supported, explicit permission state, and locally held game preferences. No assumption that provider conversation history transfers.
- **Fallback:** offline/local built-in fairy when an external service is unavailable or the player declines connection.
- **Parity:** no paid gameplay advantage for choosing a provider; accessibility, controls, and basic fairy abilities stay available to all.

## Art direction for independent contractors
Produce one small fairy identity study per task, not a whole lore bible. Show clear silhouette, color/pattern/material, movement cue, expression and one recognizable function. Distinguish provider-*inspired* proposals from officially licensed branding. Avoid provider logos, exact protected character likenesses, or implied endorsements without permission. Reuse existing asset framing/provenance conventions when source is supplied; preserve failed concepts for review. No automatic rendering dispatch.

## Engineering direction for independent contractors
Prototype a provider-neutral interface with a mock agent first. Test: connect/decline/disconnect, permission disclosure, identity swap, loss of service, and built-in fallback. Do not require credentials in the public repository; do not claim Siri/Grok/ChatGPT account connectivity until supported APIs and terms are verified. Separate visual skins from the adapter.

## Review gate
Record CANON / VERIFIED / INFERENCE / PROPOSAL / OPEN; user approval controls promotion. For any public artwork or script, identify creator and original title, source URL, applicable license, and changes. No public contractor gets private command-chain history.
