## Clock and musical meter

- New composition option: **96 beats/minute, 4/4**, because **4 bars × 4 beats × 0.625 seconds/beat = exactly 10 seconds**, and **120 uninterrupted bars = 300 seconds**. This is a deliberate *new* musical choice for this episode, not an instruction to impose an old frame on every film.
- **30 consecutive four-bar sections**, one lyric line/sung phrase per ten-second segment. The line can begin on an upbeat or sustain through the next section; do not force each line to start on a metrical downbeat. Musical phrase boundaries can flow across visual clip boundaries.
- **One voice master/score performance for 5:00**, or an owner-selected authorized complete musical recording. Do not restart song, singer identity, tuning, rhythm, or room tone every ten seconds. No thirty isolated short-song sketches.
- Not every bar is filled with words. Leave room for sung vowels, breaths, machine counterpoint, a living room, wind-like ether, and actual instrument resonance. Don't sing the shot inventory.
- Keep poetry and dramatic meaning above literal component names. The mechanical world already shows its materials.
