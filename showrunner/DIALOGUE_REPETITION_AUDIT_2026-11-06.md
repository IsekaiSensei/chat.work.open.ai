## Rule applied for future *spoken* writing

1. Stop generic announcements of process (“I'm building”; “still building”; “the structure grows”) when a new physical action can carry the story.
2. Keep `build/built/building`, `lattice`, and any recently overused distinctive noun out of new dialogue. A recurrence requires a tangible new revision **held or visible in shot** and at least **50 different spoken intervening words**. This rule counts **dialogue only**, not art-reference text.
3. Detect duplicate spoken 3–6 word phrases, repetitive rhyme endings, and overused nouns/verbs across the **whole five-minute song**, not just within one ten-second card. Detect semantically repeated actions even with novel words.
4. A refrain is not automatically acceptable because it's a song; the owner chooses any deliberate lyrical repetition. Do not make 30 fresh “songs” when the source calls for a single five-minute musical thought.
5. Restrict the **writer** revision procedure to not-yet-rendered draft lyrics. Delivered MP4s and existing approved lines are **not** overwritten by an automated auditor. The owner is sole QC authority.
