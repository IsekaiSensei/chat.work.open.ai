# A01-S01 — THE REGISTRY OF ORIGINS
## Act I, Scene 1 — screenplay / performance draft

**Location:** Court of the Solar Command Ring, interior of the GERTECH Dyson sphere.
**Time:** Four mirror rotations before Theseus and Hippolyta's joining ceremony.
**Dramatic arc:** State marriage → a father's ownership claim → a daughter's refusal → lovers' escape route → a friend's dangerous disclosure.
**Visual note:** Athenians have visibly cybernetic anatomy and a variety of manufacture and augmentation styles. Character images remain owner-selected, not prescribed by this scene. The court is working machinery; the star is visible through a lattice of moving harvest mirrors.

### Beat 1 — Four turns to a wedding

[The central star fills a vast circular window. Thousands of solar mirrors rotate. A ceremonial arch glows over two figures: THESEUS, commander reconstructed through artifice, and HIPPOLYTA, a naturally born expedition captain with powerful cybernetic augmentations. PHILOSTRATE adjusts an absurdly long wedding countdown.]

THESEUS — he:
Four more mirror turns. Every machine in the sphere knows the hour except this clock.

HIPPOLYTA — she:
The mirrors are moving at the speed they always move. You are the thing running ahead.

THESEUS — he:
I've commanded fleets into a solar storm. I've crossed the burning edge of a star. Waiting for a wedding is worse.

HIPPOLYTA — she:
You crossed my border with ships and called it courtship. Our ceremony had better improve upon your opening argument.

THESEUS — he:
Then let it be a union, not a surrender. We will flood these halls with music.

HIPPOLYTA — she:
Music for the people too, Commander. The ones who weren't born in a palace or printed with a pedigree.

THESEUS — he:
Philostrate! Open every festival channel. Let the Made sing with the Born, and the Built dance on the same floor.

PHILOSTRATE — he:
With respect, Commander, the registry assigns them separate entrances.

HIPPOLYTA — she:
Then your first wedding gift to me is simple. Remove the signs.

[PHILOSTRATE hesitates. THESEUS nods. The entrance signs blink and go dark.]

PHILOSTRATE — he:
All origins. One floor. I hope the floor has been reinforced.

[He exits to arrange the celebration. A warning chime interrupts the music.]

### Beat 2 — The ownership petition

[EGEUS enters carrying a genealogy ledger that projects a legal hologram. HERMIA follows, furious. LYSANDER, a visibly hand-built cyborg, keeps pace beside her. DEMETRIUS, impeccably fabricated and expensively upgraded, walks on Egeus's other side.]

EGEUS — he:
Commander, I bring a breach of registry law. My daughter refuses the union licensed in her name.

THESEUS — he:
A wedding complaint? Already? We haven't survived the first one.

EGEUS — he:
I commissioned her line. My own house paid for the printing. I signed the seed record, the schooling, the repair rights—

HERMIA — she:
You didn't sign my thoughts.

EGEUS — he:
And now this Built boy has loaded her mind with unauthorized affection.

LYSANDER — he:
I sent her music. If that's illegal, arrest the orchestra first.

EGEUS — he:
Demetrius is the proper match: Made to specification, fully certified, clean inheritance. Lysander is rebuilt from scavenged hardware and inherited debts.

DEMETRIUS — he:
I have offered stability, Hermia. A contract honored by the Command Ring.

HERMIA — she:
You offered me a contract. Lysander offered me a choice. You see why I prefer him.

EGEUS — he:
Commander, the Origin Charter is clear. Until her bond is transferred, the template holder may determine the union.

HIPPOLYTA — she:
That is a charter for machinery. You are speaking about a person.

EGEUS — he:
She has my name, my pattern, my registration.

HERMIA — she:
And none of those things is my heart.

[The ledger projects HERMIA's living silhouette beside the word PROPERTY. HERMIA reaches through the hologram and closes her fist. It fractures into pixels.]

### Beat 3 — The decree

THESEUS — he:
The Charter predates my command. It offers you three paths, Hermia. Accept your father's selection. Enter lifelong solitary service beyond the marriage registry. Or surrender your core to permanent shutdown.

HERMIA — she:
Is that what this sphere calls freedom?

THESEUS — he:
I told you what the law says, not what I wish it said.

HERMIA — she:
Then be its commander. Change it.

[EGEUS stiffens. HIPPOLYTA watches THESEUS carefully.]

DEMETRIUS — he:
This need not be a trial. I will give her everything the station can produce.

HERMIA — she:
It cannot produce my consent.

LYSANDER — he:
My frame is hand-built. My work keeps half your relays alive. I have no less mind, no less standing, and I am loved by the woman in question.

EGEUS — he:
Your parts have no pedigree.

LYSANDER — he:
Neither does the electricity holding your palace above the star.

HIPPOLYTA — she:
And what of Helena? Demetrius pledged a future to her before this petition.

[DEMETRIUS looks away. EGEUS closes his ledger halfway.]

LYSANDER — he:
He made promises to Helena. Now he wants Hermia because a registry stamp makes her more valuable.

DEMETRIUS — he:
My negotiations changed.

HIPPOLYTA — she:
A heart is not a procurement order.

THESEUS — he:
Enough. Hermia, you have until our ceremony. I will hear your answer after four mirror turns.

HERMIA — she:
You'll hear the same answer.

THESEUS — he:
And I hope, for all of us, that something changes before then.

[THESEUS takes EGEUS and DEMETRIUS into the private tribunal. HIPPOLYTA pauses beside HERMIA.]

HIPPOLYTA — she:
Don't confuse the Commander's silence with the entire sphere's consent.

[She follows the others. The court empties, leaving the star's low hum.]

### Beat 4 — The garden beyond the law

HERMIA — she:
He can stop a ship with one word. But he cannot stop a cruel sentence.

LYSANDER — he:
People say circuits make a heart predictable. Whoever said it never met you.

HERMIA — she:
People say love crosses every boundary. We haven't crossed even one.

LYSANDER — he:
Then let us cross a real one.

[He opens a service chart. Beneath the command ring lies a vast neglected habitat: trees, fungus, old relays, and a maintenance forest threaded through the Dyson sphere.]

LYSANDER — he:
My aunt runs an independent garden habitat beyond the Command Ring's jurisdiction. Its charter predates the Registry. We can be joined there without Egeus signing away my existence.

HERMIA — she:
How do we pass security?

LYSANDER — he:
We don't. We go through the roots. The old mycelium conduits still answer to manual tools.

HERMIA — she:
A man made of patched-together machinery proposes that we trust a tunnel full of ancient fungus?

LYSANDER — he:
I could make a speech about destiny.

HERMIA — she:
Please don't.

LYSANDER — he:
Then meet me at the garden airlock when the third mirror goes dark.

HERMIA — she:
I'll come. Not because the stars command it. Because I choose you.

[They touch foreheads. Their different interface ports briefly illuminate with the same pattern.]

### Beat 5 — Helena hears the plan

[HELENA enters, naturally born and visibly augmented with old relay implants. Her movement slows when she sees the lovers standing together.]

HELENA — she:
Of course. Even standing near one another you make the station look like it was built for two.

HERMIA — she:
Helena. You've been avoiding us.

HELENA — she:
I wasn't avoiding you. I was following a man who avoids me. It takes all my time.

HERMIA — she:
Demetrius still won't speak to you?

HELENA — she:
He speaks perfectly well when he wants a repair, a report, or an excuse. I used to be the answer he reached for. Now I'm a voice he mutes.

LYSANDER — he:
Then you deserve someone who listens.

HELENA — she:
You say that easily. You are the person Hermia hears.

HERMIA — she:
I cannot persuade him to love you, Helena. I cannot persuade him to leave me alone.

HELENA — she:
Every time you turn away, he follows. Every time I follow, he turns away. Is there a setting for that in the Command Ring?

HERMIA — she:
If there is, its maker deserves to be thrown into the star.

[HELENA laughs despite herself.]

HERMIA — she:
Tomorrow we are leaving. The garden beyond the registry. Lysander knows a way through the old conduits.

HELENA — she:
Leaving? You would abandon the whole sphere?

LYSANDER — he:
Only the part that insists it owns us. Tell no one.

HERMIA — she:
You have always been my friend. I wanted you to know why I disappear.

[HERMIA and LYSANDER exit toward the lower maintenance ring. HELENA remains, staring at the chart's fading light.]

### Beat 6 — The signal sent in secret

HELENA — she:
The Born say love is chemistry. The Made say it is design. The Built say it can be repaired. The Regenerated say it survives death.

[She touches a relay implant behind her ear.]

HELENA — she:
And I, who have all these words, cannot convince one man to look at me.

[Her implant brings up DEMETRIUS's private channel. She hesitates, then speaks.]

HELENA — she:
Demetrius. I know where Hermia is going tomorrow night.

[Far below the court, an unmapped crystal relay answers with a delicate pulse. HELENA does not hear it. Blackout.]

**END A01-S01.** Continuity out: the lovers intend to leave for the garden; Helena has initiated disclosure to Demetrius. No fairy intervention is shown yet.
