# World Book

## Start here — The Fairy is the cursor

**This is the opening explanation of TO WORLD:** the player begins with a Fairy who **is the cursor**. The Fairy's hole gives access to an infinite inventory. The player learns **CLICK → CONNECT → KEY → KEYSTROKE**: select, link, bind an input, then learn the consequence. When the Fairy enters a world, **that same Fairy becomes the in-world companion**.

Read the complete [TO WORLD — Design Foundation: The Fairy Is the Cursor (v1.0 proposed)](design-foundation-fairy-cursor.md) before the individual mechanics. Earlier introductions are retained through its **Booktracing / earlier openings** references; this new front matter takes precedence as the explanation, not as erasure of source history.
