## Original 24 technical installation lines — source dialogue, unchanged

1. "Set my aluminum chassis square. Align the mounting plates. Seat the locating pins before you tighten my frame."

2. "Install my linear guide rails. Check them for parallel. Slide the carriage through its travel before final torque."

3. "Mount my servo motor to the flange. Align the shaft coupling. Leave the screws loose until the shafts engage."

4. "Check the shaft engagement. Tighten to specification. Rotate the assembly by hand and verify smooth motion."

5. "Fit my planetary gearbox. Secure the mounting bolts. Check the output shaft for backlash and free rotation."

6. "Seat my rotary encoder. Connect the signal cable. Verify the pinout before you read my position."

7. "Install my limit switches. Set the trip positions. Check both travel limits before you enable my drive."

8. "Route my wiring harness through the frame. Keep it clear of pinch points. Leave enough slack for articulation."

9. "Strip my conductors to length. Select the proper crimp die. Compress each terminal to its specified height."

10. "Inspect the conductor crimp. Check the insulation support. Insert each contact until the housing latch engages."

11. "Mate my keyed connectors. Verify terminal retention. Apply strain relief so the contacts carry no cable tension."

12. "Mount my controller on standoffs. Handle the board by its edges. Secure the fasteners without bending the PCB."

13. "Install my DC-to-DC converter. Check the rated input voltage. Fit the specified fuse before connecting my supply."

14. "Connect my motor driver. Verify every terminal. Check the power and signal wiring against the schematic."

15. "Install my temperature sensors. Secure the leads. Confirm the readings before you command my actuators."

16. "Test my continuity. Verify my polarity. Inspect the insulation before you apply the power."

17. "Set my encoder zero offsets. Calibrate each joint. Command a low-speed motion and check my travel."

18. "Check my emergency stop. Verify the safety circuit. Reset the drive only when every fault is cleared."

19. "Enable my servo drive. Monitor the current. Apply the holding command and verify my position."

20. "Close my access panels. Secure the cable glands. Check the fastener torque and complete my inspection."

21. "My power supply is stable. My encoder signals are valid. My controller reports all systems ready."

22. "My connectors are seated. My harness is secured. My servo drives are enabled and waiting for commands."

23. "Run the homing sequence. Verify my joint positions. Store the calibrated offsets in nonvolatile memory."

24. "My installation is complete. My diagnostic checks have passed. Technician, connect the interface and bring me online."
