### DEEP-TIME ORIGIN (the reason for the series)
Gertrude is Theia, the living being at the Moon-forming collision roughly **4.5 billion years ago**. The old **65-million-year** date was explicitly corrected as an error. The swallowed alien spaceship blocks her crystalline colon, and the long living mana/alien-technology history becomes the foundation for real fairy fantasy. In the modern industrial pocket workshop, a technician mistakes biological gold-like lattice for a superconductor and performs one small physical repair that frees the blockage. Gertrude awakens. The crew needs work, rest, service and a place to gather. Read the [owner-corrected origin spine](GERTRUDE_THEIA_4_5_BILLION_YEAR_ORIGIN_2026-11-05.md).
