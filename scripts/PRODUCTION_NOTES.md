## Speech and action

- Speech target: **3–9 seconds per beat** for the current continuous-story working script; 6–9 seconds is a useful moderate-delivery preference, not a hard prohibition. Preserve archived exact words; the owner may authorize shortening or rewriting in the working version. Confirm actual readback and mouth sync.
- Label speech exactly: **she:**, **he:**, **they:**. Two speakers and bulleted scripts are supported. When one speaks, **only the named speaker mouths that line**; listeners react without voicing it. **they:** is genuinely shared speech.
- On owner-approved visual jobs, supporting workers can continue behind the speakers; silver and gold leads, with full-rainbow activity, explain the spoken idea around them. These are scene options, not a permanent cast or color requirement.
- Show real object contact when useful: tool handling, passing parts, placing probes, aligning and inserting connectors, steadying work, operating switches, clamps, or controls. A line with double meaning may reveal it refers to a component or bench task. Physical contact must serve the action; do not force contact into every shot.
- No automatic narrator, suit, palette, role, species, or character reference. The CURRENT owner-selected sources for each production win. Do not include unwanted descriptors.
- For editing, record source video, speaker-to-mouth QC, audio, timeline placement, accepted takes and pickup gaps. Mark a video DONE only with actual evidence and owner acceptance.
