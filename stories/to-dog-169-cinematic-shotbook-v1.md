# THE FIRST WORMHOLE GREW — 169-SHOT CINEMATIC SHOTBOOK

**TO WORLD / TO DOG · Cinematic conversion candidate v1 · AI-assisted public working draft**

> This is a NEW shooting/prompt companion to the already committed 169-panel story, not 169 finished cinematic paintings. The existing 44-panel and 169-panel illustrated editions remain unchanged. These shot instructions were authored as a distinct creative interpretation of the story and of a user-provided ten-second vertical video showing a ruined city, a graphite mechanical DOG, a human image on DOG's display, and a vast ember-red sky aperture.

## Production grammar

- **Format:** portrait 9:16 cinematic sci-fi; realistic dimensional lighting; high-detail matte graphite industrial surfaces, soot, fog, worn edges, cool white optics, deep blue/violet diagnostics and ember-orange hazards. State changes must also be communicated with shape, motion, or sound: never color alone.
- **DOG lock:** small adult-scale four-legged black/graphite robot with strong jointed legs, one luminous round white optic, compact geometric head, narrow cable tail, mechanical tongue tool; believable consistent mass and proportions. No cartoon ears and no costume changes.
- **Human continuity:** one unnamed adult workshop worker with work clothes, stained hands, tired physical presence; no likeness of a real named person. Another unnamed adult is the far-future asteroid farmer. THE COUNTER is a spare industrial archive interface, not a glowing evil face.
- **Mushroom continuity:** small pale biological mycelium on a damaged electronic board first; only later grow the network and apertures. 555 = pulse; amplifier = gain; fuse = protection; fungal network = fictional unknown spatial interface. Do not turn the electronics into a verified real-world wormhole recipe.
- **Visual storytelling:** one action or observation per shot; no auto-generated written captions inside generated imagery. Put DOG's diagnostic text/voiceover into the editor from the published manuscript. Do not make all 169 shots be a standing DOG looking at a wormhole.
- **Source and history:** every shot is tied by number to the original [169-panel text manuscript](to-dog-169-the-first-wormhole.md). Preserve observation vs interpretation, failed attempts, crew-return stakes and original errors. This shotbook is not implementation proof or final canon.
- **Tempo:** QUIET = 12 s; BUILD = 9 s; IMPACT = 6 s. Slow when a mistake must be understood, a human decision has consequences, or an unknown is worth looking at. Speed up for a discrete physical fault or spatial transition. Each shot can be generated separately without context from any previous generator window.
- **Edit:** allow silence, room tone, damaged bearings, relay clicks, radio static and each crystalline form's identifiable non-verbal 'ting'. Use subtitles added in post, not baked into images. Generated scenes should terminate in a stable image for matching the next clip.
