## Editing grammar — choose only as needed
1. **BASE / ACTION:** keep the ongoing technician's procedure as the persistent time-and-place spine. Return to the same completed component and hand position after every insert. The bench and equipment do not reset.
2. **CUTAWAY / CUT FRAGMENT:** an actual fairy or android render may play briefly at full opacity, then cut back into exactly the ongoing action/time. The song and story clock never restart.
3. **OVERLAY / SUPERIMPOSE:** scale and position an existing render above the working clip. Vary opacity from faint memory to opaque foreground. Show only content the owner selected and that suits that moment.
4. **INSIDE THE HEAD:** the fairy's show appears as a reflection, inset, ghost-image, screen-within-screen, or transparent mental image. This is subjective POV, NOT proof of a literal screen or a new physical fairy in the workshop.
5. **THOUGHT BUBBLE:** an image-only inset of the ongoing performance may accompany the current action. No new rendered caption or invented words; it is an editing layer (use contrast/borders rather than color alone for readability).
6. **VOICE TRANSFER / ECHO:** an already-recorded Shakespeare phrase can be carried over a different worker's reaction or lips if the existing mouth motion supports it; otherwise make it clearly off-screen/internal voice rather than faking precise lip sync. Distinguish lead fairy source voice, android quoting line, technician's spoken electrical instruction, and soundtrack.
7. **FADE / DISSOLVE:** fade layers in/out while the base action and master soundtrack continue. Do not fade to black automatically between every seven- or ten-second segment.
8. **OPAQUE INSERT / RETURN:** when an existing clip temporarily replaces the entire picture, preserve the one master timecode; on returning, the original physical action resumes without jumping backward.
9. **COMEDIC CONTEXT:** the short joke is the Shakespeare line accidentally fitting the present task or visible reaction. Never add "not like that", explanatory asides, or a rewritten punchline.
10. **LAYERED REALITY:** some echoes are actual speakers, some mental pictures, some reverberations or edited voice-over, and some superimposed images. Do not force all source clips into one literal shared room or identical role.
